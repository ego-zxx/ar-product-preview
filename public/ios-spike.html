iOS Marker-Tracking Spike

Free 6DOF tracking in iOS Safari via a printed marker (MindAR). Print or open this marker on another screen, then point the camera at it:

marker image (card.png) →

The faucet is scaled assuming the marker is printed ~8.6 cm wide (credit-card width). Judge: steadiness on the marker, and what happens when the marker leaves frame — that's the trade-off vs paid SLAM.
